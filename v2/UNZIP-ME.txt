Fenland v2 — complete folder
============================

Unzip so these files land INSIDE your existing fenland/v2/ folder,
overwriting what is there.

Two files are deliberately NOT in this zip, so yours survive:

  config.js           your live broker URLs and station settings
  test-harness.html   not part of this project

Everything else is here, including the twelve src/*.js and nine panes/*.html
that the last zip accidentally removed.

After unzipping, serve it over http rather than opening index.html directly —
MQTT cannot open a wss:// socket from a file:// origin:

  cd fenland/v2
  python3 -m http.server 8000
  # then open http://localhost:8000

New in this version
-------------------
  src/tempchart.js    24-hour air temperature trace, under the barograph
  src/windtrace.js    Beaufort spine + direction tape (updated)
  css/windtrace.css   layout for both (updated)

See README-v2.md for the full notes.
