Fixes
-----
1. Grey gust bar removed from the Beaufort tower (the red PEAK line stays).
2. The direction tape marker is now the pointer alone — the line down the
   strip is gone.
3. NE to NW no longer sweeps across the tape and back. The pointer now
   springs in the tape's own coordinates and takes the shortest visible path;
   it only jumps when the bearing genuinely crosses the seam at due south,
   where the strip ends.
4. The bottom edge of the tower's box now lines up with the bottom edge of
   the direction box. The "Force 1 - Light air" caption moved up under the
   WIND FORCE label to let the box finish on that shared line.
5. The tile panel is no longer crowded: smaller label/value/note sizes, a
   little more row height, and nothing can overflow a rule.
